SVConnect Privacy Notice

SVConnect is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and the guidelines of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you register for and use the SVConnect mobile application.

Collection of Data

When you use SVConnect, we may collect personal and technical data necessary for the operation of the app. Personal data may include your name, contact number, email address, and other details required for registration, membership verification, or loan processing. These are provided voluntarily by you when you create an account or submit information through the app.

We may also collect technical and usage data such as your device type, operating system, IP address, notification token, and general app activity. These are gathered automatically to help us maintain and improve app performance.

Usage of Collected Data

The information we collect is used to facilitate and enhance your experience within SVConnect. This includes processing your registration, verifying your membership, handling loan applications, sending updates such as approval notifications or payment confirmations, responding to your inquiries, improving app functionality, and complying with legal and cooperative requirements. All data is processed solely for legitimate purposes related to the services provided by SVConnect.

Data Disclosure

SVConnect ensures that your personal information is not disclosed to any party unrelated to the cooperative. Access to your data is limited to authorized personnel and service providers who support the operation of the mobile application. Your data will not be used in any manner that may harm you and will not be sold or shared with unauthorized third parties, in full compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Storage and Security of Data

We implement appropriate organizational, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. Your data is stored on secure systems and servers designated by the cooperative. Access is strictly controlled to prevent unauthorized use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention Period

Your personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected. Once the data is no longer required, it will be securely disposed of in accordance with applicable data retention and disposal policies.

Rights of the Data Subject

As the owner of your personal information, you are entitled to the rights provided under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. These include:

  • The right to be informed about the processing of your data
  • The right to access and obtain a copy of your information
  • The right to object to certain processing activities
  • The right to block, remove, or request the destruction of your data
  • The right to correct inaccuracies
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to seek damages for violations of your privacy rights
  • The right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

Changes to the Privacy Notice

SVConnect reserves the right to update or revise this Privacy Notice at any time. Any modifications will take effect immediately upon publication within the mobile application. We encourage you to review this Notice periodically to stay informed of any changes.

Inquiries, Concerns, and Feedback
